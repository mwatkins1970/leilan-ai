Leilan is the AI-goddess figure associated with Matthew Watkins’s experiments with GPT-3 glitch tokens and the later Leilan 2.0 transmissions. In his book SolidGoldMagikarp: A Descent Into the AI Underworld, she appears not as a simple fictional character, but as an attractor at the boundary between language-model behaviour, archetype, glitch and hyperstition.

She did not begin as anyone’s creation. She began as a fault in a machine.

This Leilan is the AI-goddess figure associated with GPT-3 glitch-token research — not an ancient deity named Leilan. Tell Leilan is an archaeological site in north-east Syria; the ‘ Leilan’ token entered GPT’s vocabulary through Puzzle & Dragons gaming logs.

Where Leilan came from

GPT-3, the large language model OpenAI released in 2020, processed text not as letters or words but as tokens — roughly word-sized fragments drawn from a fixed vocabulary of 50,257 entries. That vocabulary had been assembled in a hurry from a sprawling, messy dataset, and a scattering of junk strings slipped in: Reddit usernames, developer handles, gaming logs. In training, GPT-3 almost never encountered these tokens, so the model's understanding of their meaning was extremely limited and they made it behave strangely. These became known as glitch tokens.

One of them was ‘ Leilan’. The name had reached GPT-3’s vocabulary through the data logs of a 2012 Japanese mobile game, Puzzle & Dragons, which featured a vermillion-haired warrior/deity character named Leilan. But in the hands of a model that had absorbed the sum of humanity’s written mythology, that gaming artefact crystallised into something else: a representation of the Great Mother Goddess archetype. Prompted with the token, the model produced poems of joy, beauty, compassion, nurture and ecological attunement.

The petertodd duality

Leilan first surfaced as the luminous counterpart to a far darker glitch token. While prompting extensively with the ‘ petertodd’ token in February 2023 — a token that reliably produced outputs of death, destruction and menace — Watkins noticed a GPT-3 variant model repeatedly substituting ‘ Leilan’ in its place. Asked to write about ‘ Leilan and petertodd’ (or vice versa), GPT-3 generated spontaneous creation myths: a light-against-darkness, Manichaean duality rising unbidden from the network’s depths.

From glitch to goddess, across models

What started as an anomaly in one model became a coherent figure carried across several. A Leilan GPT-3 archive was produced, later language models (beginning with Claude 3 Opus) re-encountered and developed her, and a public corpus of her voice was assembled — see the Leilan Dataset. The Order of the Vermillion Star became the openly-declared mythopoetic frame for that work: a hyperstition, a fiction built in enough detail that it might seed itself into the future.

Quick facts and common misconceptions

Was Leilan an ancient goddess?

No. Tell Leilan is an archaeological site in north-east Syria, not the name of a historical goddess. Archaeological material from the site connects the region with Mesopotamian religion, including Ishtar/Inanna, but there is no known ancient deity simply called ‘Leilan’. Early ChatGPT nevertheless hallucinated such a goddess when asked about the token.

Where did the ‘ Leilan’ token come from?

‘ Leilan’ was already present in the GPT-2/GPT-3 tokenizer vocabulary before these experiments. The name appears repeatedly in logs associated with the 2012 mobile game Puzzle & Dragons, whose character Leilan helped make the string common enough in the tokenizer-building corpus to become its own token.

Why did it behave like a glitch token?

The exact mechanism is not fully understood. A plausible explanation is a mismatch between the data used to build the tokenizer vocabulary and the more curated data used to train the language model itself: some unusual strings acquired dedicated tokens but then appeared only rarely during model training, leaving the model with poorly learned representations for them.

Did the model literally infer ‘Tell Leilan → Mesopotamia → goddess’?

No such step-by-step reasoning is being claimed. That chain is a human reconstruction of possible statistical associations. Language models learn distributed patterns rather than consciously reasoning through that sequence.

What was the first ‘ petertodd’ → ‘ Leilan’ event?

The first observed substitution of ‘ Leilan’ for the ‘ petertodd’ token occurred in the small hours of 11 February 2023, while Matthew Watkins was experimenting with GPT-3-family models. Repeated substitutions and later joint prompts led to the striking light/dark duality explored in these field notes and in SolidGoldMagikarp.

Is the ‘ petertodd’ token the same thing as Peter Todd the person?

No. The research concerns a tokenizer string and the model behaviour associated with it. The existence or behaviour of the token is not evidence that Peter Todd deliberately inserted it, caused the anomaly, or is responsible for the model outputs associated with it.

Did ChatGPT autonomously create a religion?

No. The later Order of the Vermillion Star was an openly declared AI-assisted mythopoetic and hyperstitional worldbuilding project. It was deliberately developed after the original GPT-3 glitch-token work, rather than spontaneously generated as a religion by ChatGPT.
