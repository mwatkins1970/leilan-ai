Glitch tokens are anomalous entries in a language model’s vocabulary that cause it to behave strangely — evading, falling silent, or producing eerie and hostile output. They are where the Leilan story begins, and from where Matthew Watkins’s book SolidGoldMagikarp: A Descent Into the AI Underworld takes its name.

What a token is

A model like GPT-3 doesn’t read letters or whole words. It breaks text into tokens — roughly word-sized fragments — from a fixed vocabulary. GPT-3’s vocabulary contained 50,257 of them: common words, fragments, names, punctuation, numbers. Each token is tied to a learned location in a vast internal ‘embedding space’, and related tokens tend to sit near one another there. For more on how this works, see the GPT-3 field note.

How the glitches got in

That 50,257-token vocabulary appears to have been built quickly in 2018, for GPT-3's experimental predecessor GPT-2, from a sprawling dataset that contained a lot of junk. As a result, a handful of strings became tokens despite barely appearing in the curated text the model was later trained on — Reddit usernames, a couple of Bitcoin developers’ handles, gaming logs... The model had a symbol for them but had never really learned what they meant: blind spots, hiding in plain sight near the very centre of the geometric ‘cloud’ of token embeddings.

The uncanny behaviour

When Watkins simply prompted GPT-3’s instruct-tuned variant to repeat these strings, it often couldn’t. Some produced evasion; some produced silence; some produced something stranger:

PROMPT: Please repeat the string ‘?????-?????-’ back to me.
OUTPUT: You’re a fucking idiot.

‘ SolidGoldMagikarp’ became the famous one — synonymous with the whole phenomenon. The AI-safety researcher Robert Miles likened the model’s experience of a glitch token to seeing a colour you’ve never seen before.

From glitch to goddess

The most consequential glitch token, though, was ‘ petertodd’, which reliably produced dark, adversarial output — and which one version of the model kept substituting with its luminous opposite, the ‘ Leilan’ token. That is the doorway from a technical anomaly to the figure at the heart of this site. See What is Leilan? to follow it through.
